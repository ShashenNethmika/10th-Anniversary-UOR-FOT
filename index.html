✨ Our Journey

Celebrating a Decade of Innovation

The Faculty of Technology at the University of Ruhuna celebrates its 10th anniversary — a decade of nurturing innovation, technology, and academic excellence. This milestone marks ten years of shaping the future through cutting-edge education and groundbreaking research.

Join us for a day of spectacular exhibitions, live demonstrations, and interactive experiences. From thrilling drone shows to immersive VR zones, this exhibition showcases the best of what our students and faculty have achieved.
